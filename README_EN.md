# Persian Gulf Blockchain (PGB)

**English | [فارسی](README_FA.md)**

## 1. Introduction

**Persian Gulf Blockchain (PGB)** is an independent Layer 1 blockchain developed using **Polkadot SDK and Substrate**.

PGB is designed to provide an independent, secure, and extensible blockchain infrastructure for the creation and transfer of digital assets, tokenization, smart contracts, and real-world blockchain applications.

The network provides infrastructure for developers, businesses, organizations, and communities to build blockchain-based applications and digital services on an independent network.

## 2. Vision

The vision of PGB is to build infrastructure that connects blockchain technology with real-world economic, organizational, cultural, and social activities.

PGB originated in Iran while maintaining an open approach toward international participation by developers, validators, businesses, and organizations.

The long-term objective is to develop an independent and extensible ecosystem around the PGB network and its real-world applications.

## 3. Network Architecture

PGB is an independent **Layer 1** network built on the Polkadot SDK.

The network architecture includes:

- BABE block production
- GRANDPA finality
- Proof-of-Stake consensus
- Validator staking
- WASM-based smart contracts
- Asset and token infrastructure
- On-chain governance
- Treasury
- RPC and Explorer infrastructure
- Independent validator ecosystem

PGB is designed to remain extensible and upgradeable as the network evolves.

## 4. PGC Token

**PGC** is the native asset of the Persian Gulf Blockchain network.

PGC is designed to support network functions including:

- Transaction fees
- Staking
- Validator participation
- Governance-related activities
- Interaction with applications and services across the PGB ecosystem

Final Tokenomics, token allocation, and network economic policies will be published in the official versions of the PGB Whitepaper.

## 5. Network Use Cases

PGB is designed as a general-purpose Layer 1 infrastructure capable of supporting different blockchain applications.

Primary areas of focus include:

### Sports and Culture

- Fan tokens
- Fan participation and voting
- NFTs and digital collectibles
- Blockchain-based ticketing
- Digital ownership

### Industry and Business

- Real-world asset tokenization
- Product traceability
- Verifiable digital records
- Digital asset management
- Enterprise blockchain solutions

### Application Development

Developers can use PGB infrastructure to build applications, smart contracts, digital assets, and blockchain-based services.

## 6. Staking and Validators

Network security and block production in PGB rely on validator participation and staking mechanisms.

Validators contribute to network security, block production, and the continued operation of the network.

PGC can be used for staking and participation in the network's validation structure.

Final policies regarding Validator Bond requirements, rewards, commissions, slashing, and validator participation will be determined after evaluating network performance and the results of the Public Testnet.

## 7. Network Governance

PGB includes **on-chain governance** infrastructure.

The purpose of network governance is to provide mechanisms for the progressive management and development of the network and participation in network-related decision-making.

The final governance structure and policies will be developed and published as the network evolves.

## 8. Network Economy

The PGB network economy includes components such as:

- Transaction fees
- Staking rewards
- Validator rewards
- Treasury
- Token supply
- Inflation
- Other network economic mechanisms

Economic parameters will be evaluated throughout the testing phases to establish a sustainable model based on actual network performance, security, economic stability, and participation by users and validators.

## 9. Development Direction

PGB development follows a phased approach that includes network development and testing, expansion of the validator ecosystem, development of required tools and infrastructure, developer participation, and the creation of real-world applications on the network.

Detailed Roadmap and development phases will be presented in the official PGB Whitepaper.
